Policy Guidelines

  • Working days: Course start and end dates must be working days (Monday to Friday).
  • Public holidays: Excluded from training day calculations.
  • Half-day sessions: Available for Internal Training only.
  • Budget: External courses and certifications are subject to the employee's annual allowance.
